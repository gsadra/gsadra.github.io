Most of the interesting part of a project happens before the clean summary. There
is a first attempt that did not work, a data problem that changed the question, or
a paper that made one assumption click. Those details rarely survive into a final
write-up, but they are usually the most useful thing to share.

This page is where I will keep them.

## What belongs here

- Short notes on papers, methods, and tools I am learning.
- Design decisions and dead ends from my own projects.
- Occasional longer write-ups when a piece of work reaches a milestone.

## What does not belong here

Nothing on this page is a publication, and nothing here should be read as a
verified result. Formal descriptions of my research live on the
[research page](/research/); project write-ups live under [projects](/projects/).

## On frequency

I would rather post when there is something worth saying than keep to a
schedule. If this section is quiet for a while, it is because the work is
happening somewhere else.
